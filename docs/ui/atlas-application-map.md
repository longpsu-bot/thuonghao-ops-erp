# Atlas Application Map and Page Responsibilities

**Status:** TASK-002G source-of-need workbench map
**Authority:** UI information architecture for the active Atlas prototype

The three-stage prototype is superseded by the approved TASK-002F operations workbench. This map authorizes no backend, schema, or RPC work.

## Daily workbench

0. **Bảng điều hành** — exception-first daily control board, including upstream source blockers.
1. **Nguồn kế hoạch** — weekly menu, attendance/portions, direct requests, and pantry/internal needs before aggregation.
2. **Tổng hợp & xác nhận nhu cầu** — calculated demand, actual need, variance, and purchasing confirmation.
3. **Lập kế hoạch mua hàng** — supplier allocation, including splits and fallback.
4. **Phát hành chứng từ** — PO, dispatch, pre-issued receiving form, and reconciliation.
5. **Nhập kho & xử lý chênh lệch** — actual supplier-linked receiving and operational exceptions.

## Supporting data and governance

- Khách hàng & Trường học
- Nguyên liệu & Đơn vị
- Nhà cung cấp & Điều kiện cung ứng
- Món ăn & Công thức / Recipe Governance
- Ranh giới prototype
